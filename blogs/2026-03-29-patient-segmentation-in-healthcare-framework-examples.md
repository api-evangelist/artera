At a Glance: What is patient segmentation? Patient segmentation is the practice of grouping patients into distinct cohorts based on sharedcharacteristics: clinical condition, demographics, behavior, risk tier, or payer. Healthcare organizationsuse segmentation to personalize communication, allocate care management resources, and designtargeted interventions for high-risk groups.
